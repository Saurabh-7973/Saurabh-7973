## 👋 About me

I'm a product-minded software engineer who likes the messy middle — taking a fuzzy idea, deciding what *not* to build, and shipping something people can actually use. Most of my work lives at the intersection of **mobile apps**, **API integrations**, and **automation**.

- 🚀 **Founder of [Snapdrop](https://github.com/Saurabh-7973/snapdrop)** — a Flutter + Figma plugin tool that moves images from your phone into Figma over a QR-code session.
- 🛠 Currently building an **NSE algorithmic trading system** that runs entirely on free GitHub Actions infrastructure.
- 🧠 Working with AI dev tools daily — I treat them as a force multiplier, not a substitute for thinking.
- 🤝 Open to **full-time, contract, and freelance** roles in mobile, full-stack, or fintech automation.

### 📱 [Snapdrop](https://github.com/Saurabh-7973/snapdrop)
*Flutter app + Figma plugin*

Effortless image transfer from phone to Figma over a secure QR-code session. Built for designers who are tired of emailing screenshots to themselves.

- Figma plugin ↔ mobile app real-time pairing via Socket.IO
- 6 languages (EN, HI, ES, PT, AR, ZH) with full Flutter `intl` localization

- Polished UX: glassmorphism, blurhash placeholders, showcase onboarding, in-app review

`Dart` `Flutter` `Firebase` `Socket.IO` `Figma Plugin API`

### 📈 [algo-trader](https://github.com/Saurabh-7973/algo-trader)
*Python · zero-cost NSE trading system*

A daily-running trading bot for Indian markets that picks signals from custom **H3–H6 / L3–L6** levels with Narrow-Range-Day and Insider conditions — and places GTT orders automatically.

**Highlights**
- **Zero server cost**: GitHub Actions runs it at 9:15 AM IST every trading day
- Google Sheets as the lightweight DB (basket, levels, signals log)
- Pluggable broker layer — Angel One *and* Zerodha (Kite Connect)
- Instant Telegram alerts on every signal

`Python` `yfinance` `gspread` `GitHub Actions` `Angel One` `Zerodha` `Telegram Bot`

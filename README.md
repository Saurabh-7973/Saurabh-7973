## 👋 About me

I'm a product-minded software engineer who likes the messy middle — taking a fuzzy idea, deciding what *not* to build, and shipping something people can actually use. Most of my work lives at the intersection of **Flutter mobile apps**, **API integrations**, and **automation**.

- 🚀 **Founder of [Snapdrop](https://github.com/Saurabh-7973/snapdrop)** — a Flutter + Figma plugin tool that moves images from your phone into Figma over a QR-code session.
- ✍️ **Versatile writer on [Medium](https://medium.com/@Saurabh7973)** — Flutter performance, app-size reduction, security, and the occasional reflective essay. *(241+ followers and growing.)*
- 🎤 Invited back as an **alumni speaker** to share the journey from student to shipping engineer (Apr 2025).
- 🛠 Currently building an **NSE algorithmic trading system** that runs entirely on free GitHub Actions infrastructure.
- 🧠 I work alongside AI dev tools daily — they're a force multiplier, not a substitute for thinking.
- 🤝 **Open to full-time, contract, and freelance** roles in mobile, full-stack, or fintech automation.

### 📈 [algo-trader](https://github.com/Saurabh-7973/algo-trader)
*Python · zero-cost NSE trading system*

A daily-running trading bot for Indian markets. Picks signals from custom **H3–H6 / L3–L6** levels with Narrow-Range-Day and Insider conditions, then places GTT orders automatically.

**Highlights**
- **Zero server cost**: GitHub Actions runs it at 9:15 AM IST every trading day
- Google Sheets as a lightweight DB (basket, levels, signals log)
- Pluggable broker layer — Angel One *and* Zerodha (Kite Connect)
- Instant Telegram alerts on every signal

`Python` `yfinance` `gspread` `GitHub Actions` `Angel One` `Zerodha` `Telegram Bot`
